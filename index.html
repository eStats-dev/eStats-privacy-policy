This Privacy Policy explains what information the eStats mobile application (“eStats”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. eStats is a solar-monitoring app that displays operational data for solar power systems you are authorized to view.

01Information we handle

Account information

To sign in, you enter an email address and password. These are used only to authenticate you and to determine which solar sites your account may view. We do not use your email for marketing.

Solar system data

Once you are signed in, the app fetches and displays operational data for the solar sites linked to your account — energy production, power readings, device status, and related weather information. This data describes equipment and system performance. It is associated with your account, but it is not personal information about you and it is not collected from your device.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of data locally, using standard app storage:

  • Your Remember me preference and, only if you turn it on, your saved login details.
  • The sites and devices you have marked as favorites.
  • Whether you have already seen the introductory splash screen.

This data stays on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

02What we don't collect

eStats requests no sensitive device permissions:

  • Camera
  • Location
  • Photos & files
  • Contacts
  • Microphone
  • No advertising, anywhere in the app.
  • No selling or trading of your information.
  • No writing back to your system — eStats is read-only and cannot change how your solar equipment operates.

03How your information is used

We use the information described above only to:

  • Authenticate you and show the solar sites you are authorized to view.
  • Display your system's performance and status.
  • Remember your preferences — favorites and “Remember me”.

04Service providers

eStats uses Google Firebase as its backend to authenticate access and store the solar system data the app displays. That data is processed and stored on Google's cloud infrastructure, and Google's handling of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

We use no other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services in the app.

05Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We share it only as needed to operate the service through the provider named above, or where required by law.

06Data retention

Account and solar system data is kept for as long as your account remains active and authorized to view its associated sites. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data, contact us using the details below. Data stored locally on your device is kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

07Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information the app handles, and access is limited to accounts authorized to view their associated solar sites. Please keep your login credentials confidential. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08Children's privacy

eStats is intended for operators and owners of solar power systems and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

09Scope of this policy

This policy applies to every version of the eStats app and to everyone who uses it, however you received it. The practices described here do not change depending on how the app reached your device.

eStats is under active development, so features and the data they rely on may change over time. Any material change to how information is handled will be reflected in this policy.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Updated” date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the app after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
